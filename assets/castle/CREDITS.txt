Both player/ and enemy/ use "Castle 1" (folder "1", Asset 21/22/23) from the
pack - a classic knight-style gate tower. The two folders are pixel-identical
except the flag, which was recolored offline: blue for player, red for enemy.
See recolor-flag.ps1 (hue-selective recolor: only green flag pixels are
shifted, everything else copied unchanged).

3-severe.png was also run through fix-severe.ps1: the pack's built-in
orange/yellow "energy leak" glow around the crack read as a blood drip
(it's shaped like a downward trickle, not an upward flame) even though its
raw color was pure orange/yellow, not red. Those pixels are now recolored to
match the crack's existing near-black void, leaving a plain structural crack.
Smoke for the severe stage is drawn separately in CSS (.cg-smoke in
style.css) instead of being baked into the art.

The art's door faces left; since the enemy castle sits at the battlefield's
left edge, it's mirrored via CSS (.castle-graphic.enemy .castle-img) so its
entrance faces inward, toward the player's castle, instead of off-field.

Damage stages swapped by HP in js/render.js (castleImageStage):
1-intact.png, 2-damaged.png, 3-severe.png
