Hebrew letter+niqud pronunciation recordings used by the "nikud" exercise mode.

kamats/<letter>.mp3 (all 22 base letters): downloaded from
https://soundsofnikud.com (kamats_sounds/<translit>_kamats.mp3), a small
two-page educational site with clickable letter+niqud sound cards covering
all niqud types. Fetched 2026-07-23.

Unlike the Wikimedia Commons sources in assets/letters/ and assets/abc/, this
site has NO visible license, credits, about, or contact page anywhere
(checked the page itself, its links, and its sitemap.xml -- just two HTML
pages, no footer). Used anyway per explicit user decision, but the
authorship/license of these specific recordings is unknown. If this ever
becomes a concern, revisit finding/contacting the site owner or re-recording.

kamats/פ.wav: REPLACES the soundsofnikud.com clip above -- that one was
unusable (user reported it was unrecognizable as פ, sounded like ה). Instead,
self-made by trimming the "פ" syllable out of a real word recording:
LL-Q9288 (heb)-YaronSh-פס.wav (the word פַּס/"pas", CC0), downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons, IPA confirms hard פ ("pa"). פס was chosen because it's a
single syllable (no crowding consonant right after, unlike an earlier attempt
with פרה where the following ר made the vowel clip too short and sound like
a שווא instead of a full קמץ). Trimmed with the project's own trim-tool.html
(a small Web Audio waveform-trim utility, not part of the shipped game) by
the user, 2026-07-23.

kamats/ט.wav: REPLACES the soundsofnikud.com clip above -- user reported it
was also bad. Self-made by trimming the "ט" syllable out of a real word
recording: LL-Q9288 (heb)-YaronSh-טל.wav (the word טַל/"tal", "dew", CC0),
downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, IPA confirms /tal/. Same single-syllable
selection logic as פ above. Trimmed by the user, 2026-07-23.
