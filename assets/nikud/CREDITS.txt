Hebrew letter+niqud pronunciation recordings used by the "nikud" exercise mode.

Loudness pass (2026-07-30): every kamats/ clip (soundsofnikud.com mp3s and
self-made wavs alike) was reprocessed because the user found them hard to
hear in a noisy room. The real issue wasn't peak level (every clip already
peaked near 0dB) but that most of the site's mp3s were ~1s long with only
~200-400ms of actual sound -- the rest was silence padding at the start and
end, dragging the *average* loudness way down even though the peak was fine.
Fixed by trimming only the outermost leading/trailing silence (detected via
ffmpeg's silencedetect at -30dB, keeping a 40ms buffer so onsets/decays
aren't clipped) -- any silence strictly *inside* the clip (e.g. a
stop-consonant's natural closure, seen in ק/ת/ו) was left untouched, since
removing that would cut into real content. A small calibrated gain was then
applied per file (via `-af volume=XdB`, baked into the file, never a runtime
Web Audio GainNode -- see the abc "N" clip entry below for why) to bring
every clip's peak to a consistent -0.4dB. Net effect: most clips' mean
volume improved by roughly 5-8dB and got noticeably shorter/snappier (dead
air removed), all without clipping. Originals of all 22 files are backed up
in _raw-audio-src/_pre-loudness-backup-2026-07-30/ in case any clip needs
reverting.
(First attempt used ffmpeg's `silenceremove` filter directly, which turned
out to also strip *internal* silence periods unpredictably -- verified via
before/after duration and silencedetect comparisons, it over-trimmed ג from
190ms to 50ms and collapsed ו/ר down to fragments needing an absurd +13 to
+16dB gain to compensate. Reverted from backup and redone with a
manually-computed leading/trailing-only cut instead, which fixed all three.)

kamats/<letter>.mp3 (all 22 base letters): downloaded from
https://soundsofnikud.com (kamats_sounds/<translit>_kamats.mp3), a small
two-page educational site with clickable letter+niqud sound cards covering
all niqud types. Fetched 2026-07-23.

Unlike the Wikimedia Commons sources in assets/letters/ and assets/abc/, this
site has NO visible license, credits, about, or contact page anywhere
(checked the page itself, its links, and its sitemap.xml -- just two HTML
pages, no footer). Used anyway per explicit user decision, but the
authorship/license of these specific recordings is unknown. If this ever
becomes a concern, revisit finding/contacting the site owner or re-recording.

word-*final* (letter+קמץ followed by a silent/near-silent letter, so there's

silence marks the back edge) over word-initial. Both self-made from real
word recordings, trimmed by the user 2026-07-30, then boosted with ffmpeg
(baked into the file, all landing at peak -0.5dB, no clipping, matching

    CC BY-SA 4.0), the trailing "-gָע" syllable. +3.7dB.
