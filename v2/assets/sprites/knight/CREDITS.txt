Sprite art: "Knight" character from AutoSprite's free asset library
(https://www.autosprite.io/free-sprite-sheets/knight), licensed CC0 1.0
Universal (public domain) -- free for commercial use, no attribution
required.

Original sheets (knight-walk.png, knight-idle.png, knight-run.png, each a
5x5 grid of 384x384 frames) were sliced into individual numbered frames per
pose folder to match this project's convention. The 'blue' side is an
offline hue-shifted recolor of the original red-accented art (shifted the
red plume/cloth/shield-gem to this project's established team-blue, hue
214 deg, matching assets/castle/player's flag -- the gold/bronze armor and
dark shading were left untouched since they fall outside that hue range).

Neither 'attack' nor 'dead' has real source art: 'attack' reuses the 'run'
frames, 'dead' reuses 'idle'. See [[project_2d_board_v2]] in memory.
